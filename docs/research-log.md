# Research Log and Evidence Register

## Purpose
Record external evidence used to frame the product problem. Verify source links and publication details before citing them in public-facing material.

## Initial research leads

### ECR Retail Loss
Topic: On-shelf availability, phantom inventory, store out-of-stock versus shelf out-of-stock, inventory record inaccuracy and operational practices.
Potential relevance: supports the distinction between shelf availability and stock elsewhere in the store.
Starting point: https://www.ecrloss.com/
Status: Research lead; specific report title, date, and page/section should be recorded when used in final portfolio claims.

### Academic research on phantom inventory and inventory auditing
Topics include data-driven inspection policies, inventory record inaccuracies and replenishment decisions.
Starting points:
- https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=phantom+inventory+data-driven+inventory+inspection+retail
- https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=inventory+auditing+replenishment+point+of+sale+data+retail
Status: Search links only. Select and verify exact papers, publication years, methods, population and limitations before making specific claims.

### GS1
Topic: Product identification, master data and supply-chain visibility.
Starting point: https://www.gs1.org/
Status: General standards organisation reference. Find the exact standard or publication before attributing a specific requirement.

## Evidence rules
- Store exact title, author/organisation, publication date, URL and access date.
- Distinguish research findings from vendor claims and opinion.
- Record the geography, sample and study design when available.
- Do not apply a result from one retailer directly to all South African retailers.
- Do not present a research lead or search results page as a verified source.
- Do not copy proprietary material into the repository.
