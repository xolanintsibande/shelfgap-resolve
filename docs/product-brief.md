# Product Brief: ShelfGap Resolve

## 1. Executive summary

ShelfGap Resolve is a proposed guided investigation tool for shelf-gap cases in FMCG retail. It helps users capture what they observed, complete relevant checks, document evidence, assign corrective action, and verify the result.

The product does not claim that every shelf gap can be diagnosed from a single metric. It makes uncertainty visible and helps the user identify the next useful check.

## 2. Problem

When a product is missing from its expected shelf position, the visible gap does not reveal the cause. The product might be elsewhere in the store, the inventory record may be inaccurate, an order or delivery may be delayed, the product may be misplaced, or the evidence may be incomplete.

If the cause is assumed too early, teams may take the wrong action, repeat work, or close a case without verifying the outcome.

## 3. Target users and jobs to be done

### Primary user: Store associate / replenishment team member
**Job:** When I find a shelf gap, help me record the observation and complete the relevant checks so I know what action to take next.

### Secondary user: Store supervisor
**Job:** Help me see which cases are unresolved, who owns the next action, and which cases need escalation.

### Secondary user: Retail operations analyst
**Job:** Help me understand recurring gaps and resolution patterns using consistent case definitions and reliable data.

These are proposed personas and must be validated through interviews.

## 4. Value proposition

A structured, auditable process for investigating shelf gaps and tracking corrective action, with possible causes clearly distinguished from confirmed causes.

## 5. Product outcomes to investigate

- Faster and more consistent initial triage.
- Better completion of relevant checks.
- Clearer ownership of corrective action.
- More reliable evidence of resolution.
- Better visibility of recurring SKU-location issues.

These are hypotheses, not achieved results.

## 6. Differentiation hypothesis

Rather than acting only as an alert or dashboard, the product combines a guided checklist, evidence capture, action ownership, follow-up verification and analysis of repeat issues.

Discovery must check whether existing retailer systems already provide this workflow and whether a separate tool would add value.

## 7. Constraints

- First version uses fictional data.
- No retailer-system integrations.
- No automated orders.
- No AI diagnosis.
- No sensitive employee or customer data.
- Mobile-first, usable in a busy store environment.
- A user can record unknown or unavailable evidence.

## 8. Product principle

The system may recommend a next check based on the available evidence. It must not label a cause as confirmed unless the user records supporting evidence and the product's classification rules permit it.
