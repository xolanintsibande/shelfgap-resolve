# Pilot and Validation Plan

## Stage 1: Usability prototype
Use synthetic examples. Test whether users understand the flow and can complete it without assistance.

## Stage 2: Workflow simulation
Simulate a set of cases representing different conditions:
- Product in back room.
- Product in another location.
- System stock positive but physical stock not found.
- No stock and no recent delivery information.
- Order and receipt quantities differ.
- Evidence is incomplete or contradictory.

Evaluate whether the suggested checks are appropriate and whether the system avoids overclaiming.

## Stage 3: Real-world pilot (only with permission)
Before any pilot:
- Obtain retailer/employer permission.
- Confirm data access and privacy requirements.
- Agree baseline period and comparison method.
- Agree metric definitions and owners.
- Train participants.
- Ensure the existing process remains available if the prototype fails.

## Evaluation
Compare baseline and pilot measures. Document:
- Sample size and duration.
- Missing data.
- Differences between stores/SKUs.
- Changes in reporting behaviour.
- User workload.
- Unexpected negative effects.
- Alternative explanations.

A simple before/after change does not prove causation. If a control or comparison group is not practical, describe results as observational.

## Decision
Continue, revise, or stop based on evidence of user value, workflow fit, data quality and operational burden.
